# Swym product teardown

**Prashant Kumar** · Product Manager · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/prashant-kumar-0b069180/)

A 65-part, evidence-based teardown of [Swym](https://www.getswym.com/), the most-installed wishlist app on Shopify. It covers what the product does, why merchants pay for it, why they stay or leave, where it is defensible and vulnerable, and what I would do as its PM.

**[Read the full teardown →](teardown.md)**

## The short version

Swym is trying to turn its position as the leading Shopify wishlist into something bigger: the place where a store records what each shopper wants before they buy, and passes that signal to the store's email, text-message and in-store tools. The product is strong and proven. Its weakest point is commercial: pricing is capped by usage, and when a store runs past its cap, the wishlist is switched off on the live storefront.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core problem it solves** | Shoppers who want a product leave without buying, and the store has no record of what they wanted. |
| **Strongest feature** | The integration layer: saved-product events delivered to more than twenty marketing tools, Shopify Flow and the shop till. |
| **Weakest feature** | The usage limit, the only feature that can switch the product off for shoppers. |
| **Biggest threat** | A native Shopify wishlist in the near term; shopping through AI assistants in the longer term. |
| **North Star Metric I'd use** | Recovered saved-product orders per month. |
| **What I'd do first as PM** | Make sure a billing limit can never break a storefront, and get every new merchant to send a first message within two weeks. Then prove the extra revenue with a controlled test. |

## How it was built

- **Every important claim is labelled** FACT (a source says it directly), INFERENCE (my reasoned conclusion) or HYPOTHESIS (plausible, but not confirmable from public information). Where a number isn't public, the document says so instead of guessing.
- **Sources are listed** in a register at the end, rated by reliability.
- **Public information only.** I haven't operated a live Swym account. The workflow and UX analysis is built from Swym's help centre, release notes, App Store listings and merchant reviews.
- **AI-assisted research.** I wrote a 65-part research brief and used Claude to research and draft against it. I then reviewed, corrected and edited the result, including removing overclaims and fixing counts and prices that didn't hold up against the sources.
