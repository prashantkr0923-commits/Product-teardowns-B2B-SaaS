INFERENCE:

HYPOTHESIS:
A plausible interpretation that cannot be confirmed from public information.

# CRITICAL ANALYTICAL RULES

Follow these rules throughout the analysis.

RULE 1 — Separate facts from inference.

Explicitly label important statements as:

FACT
INFERENCE
HYPOTHESIS

RULE 2 — Do not confuse features with value.

Do not say:

"The product has dashboards."

Instead explain:

"The dashboard reduces the need for managers to manually aggregate X, which reduces Y and enables Z."

Always connect:

Feature → Behavior → Outcome → Business value

RULE 3 — Think in workflows.

A feature matters because it changes a workflow.

Always ask:

"What was the customer doing before?"

"What changes after adopting the product?"

RULE 4 — Think in incentives.

Analyze incentives of:

- End user
- Manager
- Admin
- Buyer
- Finance
- IT
- Security
- Vendor

RULE 5 — Identify trade-offs.

For major product decisions explain:

- What the company optimized for
- What it sacrificed
- Why that trade-off may have been rational

RULE 6 — Separate table stakes from differentiation.

Do not describe basic SaaS functionality as a competitive advantage.

RULE 7 — Think about second-order effects.

For major product decisions ask:

"What happens next?"

RULE 8 — Enterprise customers are not consumers.

Consider:

- Procurement
- Security
- Compliance
- Implementation
- Change management
- Training
- Stakeholder alignment
- Contracting

RULE 9 — Do not blindly trust company marketing.

Treat marketing claims as claims until supported by evidence.

RULE 10 — Never fabricate unavailable information.

If information is unavailable, say:

"Not publicly available."

RULE 11 — Prioritize customer value.

Always connect:

Customer problem → Product capability → User behavior → Outcome → Business value

RULE 12 — Be skeptical.

Do not assume that:

- More features = better product
- More customers = stronger product
- Higher price = more value
- More integrations = better product
- AI = differentiation
- Market leadership = product superiority

Critically evaluate each claim.
